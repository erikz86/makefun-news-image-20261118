# Design philosophy — "Front Page Formalism"

## The movement

**Front Page Formalism**: the news of a day, set like a broadsheet that has
been proofed a hundred times. Nothing decorative for its own sake; every mark
earns its place on the grid. The page is the artifact: a single sheet that a
picture editor can read at arm's length and a stranger can read at a glance.

## Space and form

A strict two-column grid under one masthead, divided by hairline rules. The
lead story spans the full width with a single red rule at its edge, so the
eye lands there first and then drops, column by column, down the page. White
space is not empty space: the gutters and the margins are what keep seven
stories from becoming noise. Nothing overlaps, nothing falls off the page;
every element is contained, with breathing room on all four sides.

## Color and material

Paper #faf9f5 and ink #141413 — warm paper, near-black ink, the way newsprint
actually looks. Two accents only: a deep editorial red for urgency (war,
disaster, disease) and a sober blue for diplomacy, politics and technology.
Color is the information system: red means "this is breaking", blue means
"this is the world rearranging itself". No other hues compete.

## Type and hierarchy

One serif display face for headlines, one sans for kickers and labels. The
masthead is monumental; kickers are small caps in color; headlines are bold
serif at two sizes only (lead, then grid); body copy is a single quiet size.
Hierarchy comes from size and position, never from novelty. Text is the
visual material itself — drawn, not generated — so every word stays legible
at 1024 pixels, because a front page that cannot be read is not a front page.

## Craft

The work is meticulous by construction: text is wrapped to the pixel, rules
are aligned to the grid, the footer cites its sources. A second pass refines
rather than adds — the composition is tightened until removing any element
would make the page worse. The result should feel labored over with care, the
product of someone who sets type for a living.
